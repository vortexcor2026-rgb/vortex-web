The 5 dimensions

An in-depth diagnostic, not a checklist

We evaluate 5 key dimensions of your company's marketing. Each is measured against objective criteria and compared with best practices in the market.

DIMENSION 01

Strategy and positioning

Is your value proposition clear? Is your target segment well defined? Is your positioning relevant and differentiated in your market?

DIMENSION 02

Digital presence

Website, social media, SEO and Google Business. Can your customers find you online? Does your digital image reflect your value proposition?

DIMENSION 03

Content and communication

Does your content connect with your audience? Do you have a consistent brand voice? Does your content generate conversations and conversions?

DIMENSION 04

Acquisition and conversion

How do you reach new customers? Do you have nurturing and follow-up processes? Do you know your acquisition cost and conversion rate?

DIMENSION 05

Measurement and data

Do you have Google Analytics and Search Console set up? Are you tracking the right metrics? Are decisions based on data or intuition?
