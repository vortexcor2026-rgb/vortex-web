The 6 dimensions

What APEX measures

Every dimension is scored and benchmarked against companies in your industry and market. You get a full picture of where you stand, not just what you're doing.

01

Strategy

Is your marketing aligned with your business goals? Do you have a real positioning, or are you just doing things?

02

Channels

Are you in the right channels for your audience? Are you spreading too thin or missing where your customers actually are?

03

Data and metrics

Are you measuring what matters? Do your KPIs actually tell you if marketing is working, or just if you're doing things?

04

Content

Is your content consistent, strategic and differentiated? Or is it reactive, inconsistent and forgettable?

05

Competitiveness

How do you compare to your direct competitors? Where are you winning, where are you behind, and where do the real opportunities lie?

06

Team and organization

Does your structure support your marketing goals? Are the right people doing the right things with the right tools?

Deliverables

What you get

APEX is not a report with observations. It's a structured tool built for decisions.

Marketing maturity score

A score per dimension and an overall index. Comparable across time and benchmarkable against your industry.

Failure prediction

Identification of which strategies have maxed out, which channels are under strain and which metrics are misleading you.

Competitive benchmark

Analysis of your direct competitors' digital presence, positioning and content strategy. What they're doing that you're not.

Executive roadmap

A prioritized action plan ready to present to the board or leadership team. With effort vs. impact classification for every initiative.
