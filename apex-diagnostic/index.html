6 Dimensions

What does the APEX Diagnostic evaluate?

We analyze your marketing from 6 fundamental dimensions to give you a complete and prioritized picture of where you stand and what to do next.

01

Strategy

Goals, positioning, target audience and planning. Is your marketing guided by a clear strategy or is it reactive?

02

Content and SEO

Content production, organic positioning and digital presence. How visible are you to people searching for what you offer?

03

Paid advertising

Google Ads, Meta Ads and paid channels. How well are you using paid traffic to generate real results?

04

Brand

Visual identity, consistency and perception. Does your brand reflect who you are and differentiate you from the competition?

05

Data and metrics

Analytics, tracking and decision-making. Are you making decisions based on data or on intuition?

06

Team and organization

Internal capabilities, tools and processes. Does your team have everything it needs to execute the marketing it needs?

Process

How the APEX Diagnostic works

A structured 3-week process that delivers a complete diagnosis with clear priorities and a concrete roadmap.

01

Kickoff

Initial meeting to understand your context, goals and what you already have. We define access and timelines.

02

Audit

We analyze your 6 dimensions: interviews, platform reviews, content and metrics audit. We gather the complete picture.

03

Report

Score by dimension, identified gaps and priority matrix. Everything in a visual, easy-to-read and actionable document.

04

Roadmap

90-day action plan with priorities, responsible parties and success metrics. Ready to execute from day one.

Priority gaps identified

No measurement system

No conversion tracking installed. Decisions are made without data. Quick win: Google Analytics 4 + Tag Manager in 1 week.

Undefined target audience

No documented ideal customer profile. Ads are running to broad audiences. Opportunity to cut cost per lead by 40%.

No organic content strategy

Sporadic posts without structure. Keyword analysis shows 12 high-value opportunities without competition.

Inconsistent visual identity

Outdated brand guide. 3 different logos used across channels.

90-DAY ROADMAP (EXCERPT)
  • Week 1–2: Measurement setup (GA4 + Tag Manager)
  • Week 3–4: Ideal customer profile + ad audience review
  • Month 2: Keyword content calendar
  • Month 3: Brand guide update + cross-channel consistency
